 LITTLE WARS (working title) - CREDITS
============================================================================

RULES SOURCE
  H. G. Wells, Little Wars (1913). Public domain in the United States.
  The game restates and changes its rules for a grid (GDD.md section 3);
  it includes none of Wells's text.

WHO DOES WHAT (as CLAUDE.md has it; the author may reword this)
  Kenton Howard (the author): creative direction and the design
  decisions, the story and its ink, the AI disclosure's wording,
  playtesting and releases.
  Claude (Opus 5.5): the design document from the author's direction,
  the data tables, the specs, game.html's skeleton, the headless checks
  and the docs.
  The local builder: the code inside game.html's PHASE blocks, written
  from Claude's step-by-step spec.

GAME CODE
  game.html's skeleton (2026-10-20, Claude Opus 5.5): the red banner,
  STATE AND HELPERS, and GAME FLOW - the campaign, the screens and their
  drawing, input, the conversations' wiring, Undo, the tips, the bots'
  turn playback, saving, the tables' checks and the self-test. Its 24
  PHASE blocks (PHASE 2-25, the rules and the bots) are stubs for the
  local builder to write from specs/LITTLEWARS-SPEC.txt; Claude's own
  answer code for them is kept apart, on the repository's reference
  branch, and never reaches the builder. data.js's tables (the structure
  and first values) are Claude's; their words and numbers are the
  author's to hand-edit (hand-edits.txt). ink/config.js is Claude's.
