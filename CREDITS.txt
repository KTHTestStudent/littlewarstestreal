RULES SOURCE
  H. G. Wells, Little Wars (1913). Public domain in the United States.
  The game restates and changes its rules for a grid (GDD.md section 3);
  it includes none of Wells's text.

  Claude (Opus 5.5): the design document from the author's direction,

GAME CODE
  game.html's skeleton (2026-10-20, Claude Opus 5.5): the red banner,
  STATE AND HELPERS, and GAME FLOW - the campaign, the screens and their
  drawing, input, the conversations' wiring, Undo, the tips, the bots'
  turn playback, saving, the tables' checks and the self-test. Its 24
  PHASE blocks (PHASE 2-25, the rules and the bots) were written on the
  author's laptop (2026-10-21) by local models from
  specs/LITTLEWARS-SPEC.txt, through his runner (THE RUNNER, below):
    the builder (Qwen 3.6): PHASE 2-15, 17, 18, 20-22, 24 and 25, and
      the first tries of 16 and 23;
    the fixer (Qwen 3.8): its fixes to PHASE 16 and 23, and PHASE 19
      (the builder's unfinished draft, finished by the fixer);
    Kenton Howard, by hand: one word in PHASE 18 and two lines in
      PHASE 24 (the line's "code hand-edits").
  Claude's own answer code for the blocks is kept apart, on the
  repository's reference branch, and never reached the builder or the
  fixer; a cloud Claude session (Opus 5.5) checked the built blocks
  against it. data.js's tables (the structure
  and first values) are Claude's; their words and numbers are the
  author's to hand-edit (hand-edits.txt). ink/config.js is Claude's.
