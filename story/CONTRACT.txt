SENDING AN OUTCOME (each foe_ and scene_ knot sends exactly ONE)
  VAR scene_4_outcome = "bridge"            (above the knot: the default)
  ... set it on the paths through the knot ...
  = finish
  ~ sceneDone("scene_4", scene_4_outcome)
  -> DONE
  A foe_ knot the same way: ~ foeDone("foe_4", foe_4_outcome)
  intro, tutorial and ending send nothing (knotcheck WARNs - expected).
  A last line may run into finish: + [Leave] Goodbye. -> finish
  (the game hears the outcome once that line is read). Nothing AFTER the
  outcome is sent. A second outcome is refused (the red banner says so).
  Each knot plays once a campaign: * and + choices both work.
  An unwritten knot is skipped and the game uses its DEFAULT (*).

THE KNOTS (when they play; outcomes; what each does to the NEXT battle)
  intro       a new campaign starts                    no outcome
  tutorial    before battle 0, Hook's Farm - only with   no outcome
              the tutorial on (it scores no campaign points)
  foe_1 ... foe_10   just before scene_1 ... scene_10: how their side
              plays the next battle (the author's call, 2026-10-21)
              steady         keeps together, aims at the biggest unit
              cautious       holds cover and hills, aims at guns first
              bold           rides at your back line and guns, charges
                             at even numbers
              clever         keeps its guns supported, counts a melee
                             before it charges
             *the battle's own bot: foe_1 clever, foe_2 steady, foe_3
              clever, foe_4 steady, foe_5 clever, foe_6 steady, foe_7
              clever, foe_8 steady, foe_9 cautious, foe_10 clever
              A choice of how hard, not a sidegrade: the four differ a lot.
  scene_1     before battle 1, The Two Hills, Fight to the Finish
             *scout          you see their set-up while you arrange yours
              move_first     you move first and set up in your back 3 rows
  scene_2     before battle 2, The Long Wall, Blow at the Rear
             *rear_west      raids must reach the west half of a back line
              rear_east      ... the east half
  scene_3     before battle 3, The Cottages, Defensive Game - you defend
             *scout          you see their set-up while you arrange yours
              wall           a broken wall stands across the ground in front
                             of your cottages
  scene_4     before battle 4, The Chalk Brook, Fight to the Finish
             *bridge         two more bridges over the brook
              horse_gun      your gun section is a horse gun (moves 4)
  scene_5     before battle 5, The Orchard, Blow at the Rear (west half)
             *forward        your set-up zone is your back 3 rows
              extra_troop    you have a cavalry troop more, and they move first
  scene_6     before battle 6, The Mill Hill, Defensive Game - you attack
             *more_rounds    the round limit is 2 more
              horse_gun      your gun section is a horse gun (in battle 6 it
                             already is: an open item, HANDOFF)
  scene_7     before battle 7, The Ridge, Fight to the Finish
             *head_start     you move first, see their set-up and have a
                             cavalry troop more
              extra_squad    you have an infantry squad more, and they move
                             first
  scene_8     before battle 8, The Village, Defensive Game - you attack
             *more_rounds    the round limit is 2 more
              wood           a wood stands west of the middle
  scene_9     before battle 9, The Two Fords, Blow at the Rear (whole line)
             *scout          you see their set-up while you arrange yours
              forward        your set-up zone is your back 3 rows
  scene_10    before battle 10, the finale, The Whole Floor
             *extra_troop    you have a cavalry troop more
              extra_gun      you have a gun section more
  ending      after the finale's result screen; then   no outcome
              the end screen with the campaign score (one ending)
  Each scene_ pair is a sidegrade: different kinds of help, about even (the bot
  runs check it and may change a number before the design freeze).
  Battle names are working names (data.js COUNTRY_NAMES, the author's).

THE STORY READS (the game sets these before every knot - a snapshot)
  player_side      "red" or "blue": the paint the player chose
  played_tutorial  true if Hook's Farm was played this campaign
  battles_done     battles 1-10 done: 0 before battle 1, 10 at ending
  last_result      "none", "won", "drawn" or "lost": the last battle
                   played, the tutorial included
  last_points      your points in that battle (0 if none)
  campaign_points  your total for battles 1-10 so far
  wins, draws, losses   battles 1-10 so far
  Points can end in .5 (37.5). No asking hooks: these are all it knows.
  Tags: #speaker:<key>  #end   (the box shows no pictures)
    The speaker keys and names are the writer's: list each in
    ink/config.js (names). A name stays until the next #speaker; a knot
    starts as narration; #speaker:none is narration again.

NOTES TO THE WRITER
  * The frame - whose story it is, who speaks - is yours.
  * Content (CLAUDE.md, GDD section 8): players are "players" or "you";
    Red and Blue are paint colours, not peoples - the same treatment for
    both, neither the villain; no real nations, wars, regiments or people;

    stays out of the game.
