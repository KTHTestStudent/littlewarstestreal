============================================================================
 LITTLE WARS - HAND EDITS: the guide to data.js (from the skeleton,
 2026-10-20)
 Every word the player sees and every number worth tuning is in data.js,
 in plain tables; game.html only reads them. Open data.js in VS Code, change
 the words or numbers inside the quotes or after the colons, save, and
 press F5 in the browser.
============================================================================

TWO KINDS OF EDIT
  SAFE  words (names, log lines, screen text, tips, colours). The game
        changes; the self-test stays green (node dev/selftest.js still says
        SELF-TEST 42/42 PASS), because its probes read the words from the
        tables.
  RULE  numbers, maps, armies, battles, outcomes, bot styles. The game
        plays differently, so the self-test notices ON PURPOSE: probes that
        write the old numbers fail, and the replay lines change. That is
        not a bug - it tells a Claude session to bring the probes, the
        spec's CHECK lines and the GDD in step (cloud work), or you can try
        values first and let the next cloud session catch up.

IF THE PAGE GOES WRONG AFTER AN EDIT
  A plain screen saying "data.js did not load all the way" means a slip in
  data.js: a missing comma, quote or bracket. Press F12, then Console: the
  red line names data.js and the line (the slip is on it or the line
  above). A red banner at the top that starts "data.js" names the table and
  what it needs (for example "LEVELS line 3: type must be finish, rear or
  defend.").
  Keep to the shape of each table: one record per line, the same keys in
  the same order, double quotes, a comma after every line but the last.
  Inside quotes, a quote mark needs a \ in front: \".

THE TABLES, IN FILE ORDER
  RULES  (RULE)  the rules' numbers (GDD 3.10): gun range, shots, crew,
         cover %, isolation distance, prisoners per escort, escort walk,
         raid size (a raider must stand on the back line through one
         enemy turn), withdrawal turns, the Defensive Game's throughSize
         (5: only an infantry squad gets through - the author's decision
         14; the bots' cavalry also read raidSize and throughSize to decide
         whether to ride for a back line) and quarter %, and the points
         (win, draw, a gun, a prisoner). Whole
         numbers of 1 or more, except cover and quarter (percents, 0-100)
         and prisonerPts (0 or more).
  ON_TARGET  (RULE)  how often a shot lands on the aimed square, by range:
         {upTo, pct}, nearest band first; each upTo bigger than the last.
  ARMS  (RULE)  each arm's figures, move and points per figure. Keep the
         five keys (inf, cav, gun, hgun, esc) and gun: true / false as
         they are.
  LOSS_ORDER  (RULE)  the order a melee takes losses in (GDD 3.6): own for
         the side whose turn it is, other for the other side. Each list
         keeps all five arms, once each.
  PIECES  (name SAFE; the rest RULE)  the map letters. name is shown when
         a square is pointed at (about 16 letters). look is how it is drawn
         (floor, wood, hill, house, church, wall, rock, river, ford,
         bridge); cost, enter, blocks, cover, hill, ford are the rules. A
         new named house (a new letter) is a new line.
  COUNTRIES  (RULE)  the maps: rows of PIECES letters with a space between
         them, their back line at the top, yours at the bottom; every row
         as long as the others; at most 18 columns and 12 rows. Keep each
         battle's set-up rows clear for its ARMIES (the red banner names a
         unit on a square it can't stand on). Battles 1-10 are rough first
         drafts for the bot runs to reshape.
  COUNTRY_NAMES  (SAFE)  the battles' names (about 22 letters). Working
         names: rename any.
  SWITCHES  (RULE)  the squares a story outcome's ["piece", key] changes:
         {country, key, at, piece}. at is a square name ("C4").
  LEVELS  (RULE)  the eleven battles, in order: type, country, who moves
         first, the curtain, each side's set-up rows, the round limit, the
         draw line, their bot's style, a raid's half, the defender, the
         two knots before it (foe: the one that picks their bot; knot: the
         scene_ knot), and the target win rate. Keep lv in order (0-10).
  ARMIES  (RULE)  every unit of every battle with its square (and the
         gentle bot's goal square). A side's units of one arm get their
         names in this order.
  EFFECTS, DEFAULTS  (RULE)  what each scene_ knot's outcomes do to the
         next battle, and the default used while a knot is unwritten; and
         each foe_ knot's bot styles (its default is the row's bot, so it
         has no DEFAULTS line - keep that style among its outcomes). The
         outcome NAMES are also in story/story.ink, ink/config.js and
         story/CONTRACT.txt: rename one in all four places (a cloud session
         can do it).
  BOT_STYLES  (RULE)  the bots: how a gun aims, when a unit charges, and
         the points a bot gives a square (advance, cover, together, groups,
         hunt, hold, guard, slip). The comment above the table says what
         each does. Tuning a bot is a data change.
  UNIT_NAMES  (SAFE)  the units' names (about 12 letters). Keep at least as
         many names as a side ever has of that arm (5 inf, 4 cav, 3 gun).
  LOG_WORDS  (SAFE)  the log's lines (about 70 letters). Keep the {names}
         in braces: {n}, {a}, {b}, {max} are numbers, {name} a unit,
         {country} a battle, {type} a battle type. holdYou and holdThem
         are Claude's drafts (2026-10-21) for a raider arriving on the
         back line, one turn before its raid counts - the author's to
         word.
  SCREEN_WORDS  (SAFE)  the words on every screen, one group per screen.
         A letter in brackets - (E), (U), (L) - is the key that does it;
         the key itself is in the code, so keep the letter. The title is
         here too ("Little Wars", a working title). The AI disclosure is
         NOT here: it stays in game.html (dev/disclosure.js reads it there).
  TIPS  (SAFE)  Hook's Farm's tips, each shown once when it first matters
         (about 3 lines of 60 letters).
  THEME  (SAFE)  colours ("#rrggbb"), and the shapes that tell the sides
         apart without colour: hat "tall" or "dome", base "ring" or "bar".
         Give Red and Blue different shapes.
  DATA_OK = true  the last line - keep it last.

AFTER A RULE EDIT (a cloud session, or you)
  node dev/selftest.js             which probes and replay lines changed
  node dev/bots.js . 200           win rates, rounds and actions per battle
                                   against GDD section 5's targets
