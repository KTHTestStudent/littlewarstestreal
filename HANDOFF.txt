THE AUTHOR'S DECISIONS (2026-10-20)
  1 The game: a grid-tactics campaign after H. G. Wells's Little Wars, with
    Red and Blue toy soldiers. Nothing from Garden Defense and none of the
    Louis cast.
  2 Content: nothing of Wells's sexism, colonialism or national rivalry
    (GDD "Look and content"; CLAUDE.md, content rules).
  3 Design: GDD section 10 (11 battles; seeded gunfire around an aimed
    square; squad-sized units; no timed moves; the player picks a side, and
    the sides are symmetrical; points carry over only; no Phaser; the
    tutorial's knots send no outcome).
  4 Branches: working is his; Claude pushes only to working-claude; main is
    the front page.

    story itself.
